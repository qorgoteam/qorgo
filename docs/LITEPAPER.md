# QORGO Litepaper

## 1. What is QORGO?

QORGO is a meme culture project centered on a fictional green character called Qorgo, the receipt goblin.

Qorgo works in the fictional:

**Department of Unnecessary Internet Finance**

His job is simple:

Question the hype.

Ask for evidence.

Show the receipts.

## 2. The Brand

QORGO combines:

- meme culture
- internet culture
- community
- transparency
- crypto education
- on-chain verification

The goal is to make transparency culturally shareable without making it boring.

## 3. The Character

Qorgo is a green office goblin carrying:

- a calculator
- receipts
- a red folder

His signature question:

> WHERE ARE THE RECEIPTS?

## 4. Community

Community activity may include:

- memes
- creator content
- X discussions
- Spaces
- community events
- educational material

## 5. Token

$QRGO is the planned digital asset associated with the project.

The token is currently not deployed.

Final token and contract information will be published after verified deployment and security review.

## 6. Security Philosophy

QORGO aims to make important information publicly verifiable.

That includes, where applicable:

- contract source
- deployment transaction
- token supply
- liquidity information
- official wallet information
- security reviews

## 7. What QORGO Does Not Promise

QORGO does not promise:

- profits
- returns
- market performance
- exchange listings
- liquidity
- future token price

## 8. Risk

Crypto assets and memecoins are highly speculative and may lose substantial or all of their value.

Users should conduct their own research and seek appropriate professional advice.

## 9. Core Message

# SHOW RECEIPTS, NOT PROMISES.
