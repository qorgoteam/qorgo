# QORGO Security

## Official Security Policy

QORGO will never ask users for:

- seed phrases
- private keys
- passwords
- wallet recovery phrases
- funds through random direct messages

## Contract Verification

The $QRGO token is currently not deployed.

There is currently no official contract address.

Once a contract is deployed and verified, its official address and transaction information will be published through the official QORGO channels.

## Scam Prevention

Do not trust:

- unofficial contract addresses
- random DMs
- fake admins
- fake giveaways
- fake partnership announcements
- screenshots without verification

When in doubt:

STOP.

VERIFY.

## Reporting

Suspicious links, impersonation accounts and security issues should be reported through the official QORGO community channels.
